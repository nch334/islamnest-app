What IslamNest does

  • Prayer times

    Calculated on your phone for your location. IslamNest picks a common method for your region, and you can choose another method, Asr school or minute adjustments to match your mosque.

  • Azan alerts

    Choose Full Azan, a short alert, silent or off for each prayer in the apps. Alerts are scheduled on your phone.

  • Qibla compass

    See the Qibla direction from where you are. The live compass uses your phone's motion sensors, and readings stay on your phone.

  • Mosques near you

    Find mosques nearby using OpenStreetMap data, then get directions. Details may be incomplete, so please confirm them locally, and suggest corrections for review.

  • Quran and remembrance

    Read the Quran with named sources, and use duas, athkar, a tasbih counter and the Names of Allah.

  • Zakat and giving

    Work out Zakat, Sadaqah and Fitrana, then visit official charity websites. IslamNest does not collect or process donations.

  • Sunni and Shia

    Choose your school of thought. The Shia Ja'fari home offers Ja'fari prayer times and Shia resources.

  • Ask AI

    Ask questions and get answers that cite IslamNest's library. Answers are AI-generated, not religious rulings. Please consult a qualified scholar.

  • Also included

    Halal places, Islamic news from named sources, and English, Arabic, Urdu and several other languages.

Questions and answers

Are the apps available yet?

Not yet. They are being prepared for Google Play and the App Store. Store links will be added to this page only when the apps are live.

Is IslamNest free?

Yes. IslamNest is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

How are prayer times worked out?

They are calculated on your phone for the location you choose. Calculated times can differ from your mosque's timetable, so check with your mosque for congregation (iqamah) times.

Will the Azan sound when the app is closed?

On Android, yes: alerts for the coming days are saved on your phone and can ring with the app closed and without internet. On iPhone, Apple limits notification sounds to 30 seconds; tap the alert to open IslamNest and play the full Azan.

Do I need an account?

No. Prayer times, the Qibla, mosques, the Quran and the calculators work without one. An account lets you keep settings and saved items across devices, and is needed for Ask AI.

What happens to my location?

Your location is rounded to about 1 km before it leaves your phone. It is saved with your account only if you are signed in. You can search for a city instead of sharing your location.

Does IslamNest take donations?

No. IslamNest helps you calculate what you give and links to official charity websites. You never enter card details in IslamNest.
