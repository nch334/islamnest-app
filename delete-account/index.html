Delete your IslamNest account

This page explains how to delete the account you use in the IslamNest app and on islamnest.com. It is one account for both.

How to delete your account

  1. Go to islamnest.com/my-data in any browser, or open My data in the app.
  2. Sign in with the account you want to delete. If you signed in more than 15 minutes ago, you will be asked to sign in again for your safety.
  3. Under Delete account, type DELETE to confirm.
  4. Tap Delete my account permanently. You will see "Your account has been deleted." when it is done.

Deleting your account cannot be undone.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, profile and the personal records linked to it, including:

  • settings and preferences, including Azan settings saved to your account
  • Quran bookmarks, reading progress and goals
  • saved Zakat calculations and your giving log
  • prayer logs, fasting logs, tasbih sessions and saved duas
  • AI chats and dream conversations saved to your account
  • favourites, follows and news preferences
  • your marketplace profile and listings, if you had any
  • corrections and reports you sent
  • applications and listings that staff have not yet approved

What is kept, without your name

Some records other people rely on stay in IslamNest, but they are no longer linked to you once your account is deleted:

  • approved organisations and halal listings, and applications staff have already reviewed
  • organisation suggestions, events, schedules and sources you contributed

If you managed a verified mosque page, that mosque becomes unclaimed again.

Data saved on your phone

Some settings, such as your location and prayer settings, are also stored on your phone or in your browser. When you delete your account, IslamNest also clears this data on the phone or browser you used. Other phones or browsers where you used IslamNest keep their own copy: clear IslamNest's data in that phone's settings, uninstall the app, or clear the site data for islamnest.com in that browser.

If you cannot sign in

Use the contact form and ask for your account to be deleted. Use the email address of the account so we can find it.

Staff accounts cannot be deleted this way and are removed by another administrator.
